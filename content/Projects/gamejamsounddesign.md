title: Game Jam Sound Design
---

I've long held an interest in sound design.   I am very aware that developing a good sound design can make or break movies or games.  Good sound design sets the right mood and tone.   It can make the visuals seem more immersive.   Poor design can make things feel very bland or unfinished.

My personal rule for participating.   I am not using generative AI to create any of these sounds.  

![LD58 - This Rocks](/images/posters/thisRocks.png)

Ludum Dare 58 was my first attempt at contributing sound design to a game jam.   The theme for that game jam was "collector".   The team decided to develop a game that had a character walking along the beach looking for interesting rocks.   The game can be found [here](https://ldjam.com/events/ludum-dare/58/this-rocks)

![LD59 - Almost Empty](/images/posters/almostEmpty.png)

Ludum Dare 59 had the theme "signals".   The team developed a game where your character flies from planet to planet delivering packages.   The game can be found [here](https://ldjam.com/events/ludum-dare/59/almost-empty)

![LD60 - Rise from the Heap](/images/posters/riseFromTheHeap.png)

Ludum Dare 60 had the theme "old tech".   The team developed a game where an old computer is trying to survive within a junk heap.    The game can be found [here](https://ldjam.com/events/ludum-dare/60/rise-from-the-heap)
